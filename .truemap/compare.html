truemap and other tools

Each open-source tool nearby covers one stage of working with an agent. truemap puts the plan, the change and the check on one map that lives in the repo. Facts about other tools come from the research in docs/VISION.md (2026-11-19), not re-checked since.

Where truemap is weaker

  • No real-time editing by several people at once; Nimbalyst and Chopin have it.
  • Nimbalyst's decision blocks offer six question types; truemap's ask is a single choice.
  • The bet that people will direct agents on a map, not only in the terminal, is not yet proven.
